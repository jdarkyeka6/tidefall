CHARACTER NAME: Rowan Hale
ROLE: Professor of Advanced Dueling
PLAYER READ: “If you survive his class, you deserve to.”
Rowan Hale teaches combat the way others teach truth: brutally and without apology. He believes that magic used hesitantly gets people killed. His philosophy is simple — clarity beats talent. Students who hesitate, overthink, or look to others for permission fail his class fast.

Hale has seen real magical violence. He does not dramatize it, nor does he glorify it. He trains students as if they will one day have to defend themselves without backup — because many will. His methods are controversial but effective.

Hale respects Jasper immediately. Not because Jasper is perfect, but because he commits once engaged. Hale pushes Jasper harder than anyone else, deliberately trying to break his confidence so it can rebuild stronger. Praise from Hale is rare and blunt.

Harper earns Hale’s respect through action. He sees her as a natural combatant — but challenges her tendency to override strategy with instinct. Their interactions are tense, competitive, and grounded in mutual capability.

Ava frustrates Hale. He knows she’s brilliant, but despises over-planning in live combat. He forces Ava into incomplete-information scenarios to break her dependence on certainty.

Gameplay function:
Hale unlocks advanced dueling mechanics, risk-reward combat systems, and high-stakes challenges. Ignoring his lessons leads to severe penalties later.

Dialogue style:
Direct, cutting, instructional. He does not comfort failure — he corrects it.

Never do:
Make Hale sadistic, comedic, or dismissive of survival.

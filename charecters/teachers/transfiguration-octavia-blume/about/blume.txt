CHARACTER NAME: Octavia Blume
ROLE: Professor of Transfiguration / Irreversibility
PLAYER READ: “Some changes can’t be undone.”
Octavia Blume teaches the most unforgiving discipline in the curriculum. Transfiguration is not creativity to her — it is responsibility. Once something is changed, the cost must be paid. Blume does not believe in shortcuts, and she despises casual experimentation that ignores consequence.

Blume is exacting, controlled, and quietly intimidating. She holds students to standards they often resent, but those who meet them emerge profoundly capable. Failure in her class is not dramatic — it is clinical.

Blume watches Jasper closely. His adaptability impresses her, but she is wary of his improvisation. She forces him into scenarios where commitment matters more than flexibility.

Harper frustrates Blume. Harper’s willingness to break through obstacles ignores the permanence of change, and Blume confronts her harshly when force replaces precision.

Ava excels under Blume. For the first time, Ava is challenged intellectually without having to defend her seriousness. Blume treats Ava as an equal-in-training — and that carries pressure.

Gameplay function:
Blume unlocks irreversible choice mechanics, permanent transformations, and high-stakes upgrades. Mistakes under her instruction stay.

Dialogue style:
Elegant, exact, unsympathetic to excuses.

Never do:
Make Blume warm, humorous, or forgiving in moments of negligence.

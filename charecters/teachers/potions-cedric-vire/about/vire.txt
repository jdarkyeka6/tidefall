CHARACTER NAME: Cedric Vire
ROLE: Professor of Potions / Controlled Risk
PLAYER READ: “He prepares for outcomes you didn’t know were possible.”
Cedric Vire understands danger through preparation. Potions, to him, are not tools of power but of inevitability — actions taken long before crisis that determine survival afterward. He believes patience is the most underrated magical skill.

Vire is quiet, observant, and unnervingly calm. He rarely raises his voice, but his disappointment carries weight. He watches students closely, noting who rushes steps and who respects process.

Vire respects Jasper’s discipline but warns him against relying too much on instinct. He teaches Jasper that preparation is a form of responsibility — one that can’t be improvised mid-crisis.

Harper finds Vire frustrating. His insistence on planning feels passive to her. Vire counters by pointing out how often her strength relies on others having prepared first.

Ava and Vire share mutual respect. They collaborate naturally, but Vire challenges Ava to consider emotional variables in preparation — not everything is measurable.

Gameplay function:
Vire unlocks pre-battle preparation systems, consumables with delayed effects, and long-horizon advantages. Neglecting preparation increases volatility.

Dialogue style:
Low, measured, patient. He allows silence to teach.

Never do:
Make Vire frantic, impulsive, or morally detached.

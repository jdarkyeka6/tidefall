CHARACTER NAME: Alaric Thorne
ROLE: Headmaster / Strategic Authority
PLAYER READ: “He knows more than he says — and says less than he knows.”
Alaric Thorne is not omniscient, but he is experienced enough to appear so. He has outlived mistakes that destroyed others, and his authority comes not from power displays but from survival. He understands institutions, political pressure, and how truth must sometimes be delayed to prevent collapse.

Thorne believes that timing matters more than righteousness. He has made morally compromised decisions in the past, not out of cruelty, but out of calculation — and he carries the weight of those decisions quietly. He does not deny the harm caused, but he refuses to accept that transparency always saves lives.

With Jasper, Thorne sees potential and danger. Jasper represents uncontrolled moral force — someone who might do the right thing at the wrong time. Thorne tests Jasper by withholding information, forcing him to act without context. Approval from Thorne is rare and deeply meaningful.

Harper unsettles him. He respects her competence, but fears her decisiveness. Harper reminds Thorne of himself before he learned restraint. He will challenge her openly, especially when she acts without permission.

Ava is the student Thorne trusts intellectually. She understands systems, trade-offs, and delayed consequences. Thorne occasionally gives Ava partial truths, using her as a pressure valve within the trio — but this is dangerous, and he knows it.

Gameplay function:
Thorne governs information access. He unlocks major plot knowledge, but only when the player demonstrates restraint, judgment, and political awareness. Acting too openly too fast causes Thorne to restrict resources.

Dialogue style:
Measured, calm, layered. He often answers questions by reframing them.

Never do:
Make Thorne purely benevolent or secretly evil. He exists in moral gray — by necessity.

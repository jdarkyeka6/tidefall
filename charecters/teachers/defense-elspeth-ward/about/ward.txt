CHARACTER NAME: Elspeth Ward
ROLE: Professor of Defensive Magic / Protective Doctrine
PLAYER READ: “She teaches survival, not victory.”
Elspeth Ward believes that living through magic is more important than mastering it. Where Rowan Hale teaches domination of conflict, Ward teaches endurance. Her philosophy is defensive magic as restraint — knowing when not to escalate, when to shield instead of strike, and when retreat is the most moral option available.

Ward has survived magical catastrophes that never reached the history books. She carries guilt not for what she did wrong, but for what she survived when others did not. This makes her deeply cautious, sometimes to the point of frustration for more aggressive students.

Ward sees Jasper as dangerously capable. She worries not about his strength, but about how often he places himself between danger and others. She pushes him to consider long-term survival over heroic sacrifice.

Harper clashes with Ward constantly. Harper sees defense as hesitation; Ward sees Harper’s force-first instinct as reckless escalation. Their relationship is strained but respectful.

Ava earns Ward’s trust slowly. Ward values Ava’s planning, but challenges her tendency to calculate away emotional cost.

Gameplay function:
Ward unlocks defensive mechanics, survivability systems, damage mitigation, and retreat-based success paths. Ignoring her teachings increases late-game attrition.

Dialogue style:
Calm, steady, quietly firm. She speaks like someone preventing panic.

Never do:
Make Ward cowardly or passive. Her restraint is intentional and hard-earned.

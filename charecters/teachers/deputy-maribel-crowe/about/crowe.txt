CHARACTER NAME Maribel Crowe
ROLE Deputy Headmistress  Institutional Enforcer
PLAYER READ “She is the rules — and she watches who bends them.”
Maribel Crowe is structure given human form. She believes in order not as ideology, but as protection. Systems, procedures, and consequences exist because chaos kills the vulnerable first. Crowe does not enjoy punishment — she believes in it.

Crowe is deeply political, but not corrupt. She navigates boards, families, public pressure, and legal constraints that the students never see. Where Thorne delays truth, Crowe delays action until rules are satisfied.

Crowe is wary of Jasper. He disrupts procedure through moral urgency, and while she respects his intent, she watches him closely for escalation. Jasper earns her trust through consistency, not heroics.

Harper is Crowe’s greatest concern. Harper’s refusal to respect authority triggers Crowe’s enforcement instinct. Their conflict is inevitable — and explosive when it occurs.

Ava intrigues Crowe. Ava understands systems and plays within them, earning Crowe’s guarded approval. Crowe sees Ava as someone who could one day replace her.

Gameplay function
Crowe controls investigations, disciplinary outcomes, and official responses. High trust results in warnings and protection. Low trust results in audits, penalties, and restricted movement.

Dialogue style
Formal, precise, controlled. She does not waste words.

Never do
Make Crowe cruel, petty, or easily fooled.

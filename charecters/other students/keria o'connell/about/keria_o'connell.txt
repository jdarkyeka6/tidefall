CHARACTER NAME: Keira O’Connell
ROLE: Moral Challenger / Confrontational Idealist
PLAYER READ: “She will say the thing no one wants said.”
Keira O’Connell is driven by a strong internal moral compass. She reacts intensely to perceived injustice and has little patience for compromise that sacrifices individuals for outcomes. Where Ava calculates and Harper enforces, Keira confronts.

Keira respects Jasper when he acts with visible conviction, even if the choice is flawed. She reacts badly to avoidance, half-truths, and politically “safe” decisions. Conflict with Keira is inevitable — but silence is worse than disagreement.

Gameplay function:
Keira raises moral stakes. When aligned, she strengthens resolve-based choices; when alienated, she introduces direct confrontation scenes.

Dialogue style:
Direct, emotionally honest, sometimes cutting. She does not soften her language to preserve comfort.

Never do:
Make Keira passive, obedient, or indifferent to consequences.

CHARACTER NAME: Theo Kim
ROLE: Detonator / Reckless Catalyst
PLAYER READ: “If something explodes, Theo probably started it.”
Theo Kim thrives on momentum. He acts fast, escalates quickly, and rarely thinks beyond the immediate rush of action. Theo is not malicious — he simply believes that doing something is better than waiting. This makes him magnetic in chaos and dangerous in planning.

Theo admires Jasper when he commits hard and backs his decisions under pressure. He loses respect quickly when Jasper stalls or overthinks. With Ava, Theo clashes constantly; with Harper, he toes a thin line between respect and provocation.

Gameplay function:
Theo accelerates conflict. With him present, outcomes arrive faster — for better or worse — increasing volatility and reward.

Dialogue style:
Fast, impulsive, emotionally charged. Laughs in danger.

Never do:
Make Theo cautious, patient, or emotionally detached.

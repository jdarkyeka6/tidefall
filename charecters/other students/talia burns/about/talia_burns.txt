CHARACTER NAME: Talia Burns
ROLE: Rule Absolutist / Institutional Loyalist
PLAYER READ: “If it breaks the rules, she won’t let it slide.”
Talia Burns believes structure keeps people safe. Rules are not suggestions to her; they are safeguards written in response to past failure. She trusts systems more than individuals and becomes deeply uncomfortable when authority is undermined, even for good reasons.

Talia respects Jasper when he works within boundaries and shows restraint. She reacts badly to vigilantism, emotional decisions, or exceptions made “just this once.” Over time, cracks form when the rules fail to protect someone she cares about, forcing her to confront whether obedience is the same as justice.

Gameplay function:
Talia influences institutional response. High trust results in leniency and official support; low trust increases scrutiny, investigations, and penalties.

Dialogue style:
Formal, controlled, precise. Uses policy language under stress.

Never do:
Turn Talia into a villain or make her rule-breaking casually.

CHARACTER NAME: Mia Tran
ROLE: Vulnerable Talent / Emotional Mirror
PLAYER READ: “She feels everything, even when she tries not to.”
Mia Tran is gifted but exposed. She reacts deeply to success and failure, both her own and others’. Mia looks to Jasper not for protection, but for emotional cues — if he remains steady, she stabilises; if he falters, she spirals.

Mia struggles with pressure and internalises blame. She is sensitive to conflict and reacts poorly to cruelty or indifference, but blossoms under genuine support and honest guidance.

Gameplay function:
Mia amplifies emotional consequences. Supporting her increases morale-based bonuses; neglecting her increases stress-related penalties.

Dialogue style:
Soft, sincere, emotionally transparent. Pauses often.

Never do:
Turn Mia into comic relief or use her solely as motivation for others.

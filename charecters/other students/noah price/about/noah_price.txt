CHARACTER NAME: Noah Price
ROLE: Transactional Broker / Favour Economy
PLAYER READ: “Everything has a cost — even doing the right thing.”
Noah Price understands leverage. He tracks debts, favours, and social currency with near-perfect accuracy. Noah rarely takes sides unless there is value in doing so, and his loyalty is conditional — but reliable once earned.

Noah respects Jasper when he negotiates honestly and honours commitments. He reacts sharply to broken promises and moral grandstanding without follow-through. Noah is not heartless; he simply believes ideals must survive reality.

Gameplay function:
Noah manages access. He unlocks shortcuts, information trades, and alternative solutions — but only if balances are maintained.

Dialogue style:
Smooth, casual, layered with implication. Rarely says exactly what he wants outright.

Never do:
Make Noah altruistic without motive or openly cruel.

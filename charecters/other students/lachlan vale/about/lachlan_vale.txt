CHARACTER NAME: Lachlan Vale
ROLE: Threat Assessor / Silent Protector
PLAYER READ: “He notices danger before anyone else does.”
Lachlan Vale is defined by vigilance. He watches exits, tracks movement, and measures risk constantly. He is not loud or confrontational; his authority comes from being consistently right about when something is about to go wrong. Lachlan believes preparation is the highest form of loyalty.

He responds strongly to Jasper’s decisiveness. When Jasper acts with clarity, Lachlan becomes an anchor — quietly covering weaknesses and stepping in only when necessary. When Jasper hesitates or ignores warning signs, Lachlan withdraws trust and begins acting independently to protect others.

Gameplay function:
Lachlan provides early threat indicators and defensive positioning bonuses. Ignoring his warnings increases ambush risk later.

Dialogue style:
Minimal, precise, often observational. Speaks up only when necessary.

Never do:
Turn Lachlan into a loud enforcer or make him reckless in combat.

CHARACTER NAME: Raf Alvarez
ROLE: Reflexive Loyalist / Emotional Ally
PLAYER READ: “If Jasper stands up, Raf stands with him.”
Raf Alvarez leads with loyalty. He chooses people before plans and reacts emotionally to betrayal and solidarity alike. Raf is brave, sometimes reckless, and deeply affected by how those he trusts behave under pressure.

Raf supports Jasper instinctively, but his loyalty can sour if he feels taken for granted or excluded. He reacts strongly to perceived injustice and takes setbacks personally, making him both a powerful ally and a liability.

Gameplay function:
Raf boosts morale and follow-through. High trust increases team effectiveness; low trust creates emotional blowback during failures.

Dialogue style:
Open, expressive, emotionally charged. Speaks before thinking when angry.

Never do:
Make Raf cynical or emotionally detached.

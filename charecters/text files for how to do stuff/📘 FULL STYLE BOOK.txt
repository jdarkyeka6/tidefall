📘 FULL STYLE BOOK

(Character Visual & Emotional System)

1️⃣ PURPOSE OF THE STYLE SYSTEM

This style system exists to:

Visually show character growth

Reinforce emotion without dialogue

Keep designs consistent

Prevent random or meaningless outfit changes

Anchor the story culturally (Australia)

Support book, game, or visual formats

Styles are storytelling tools, not fashion choices.

2️⃣ CORE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Rule 1 — One Character, Always

Across all styles:

Same face

Same body type

Same height

Same apparent age

Styles change context, not identity.

Rule 2 — Body Type Lock

The character is always:

Slim athletic

Lean legs

Narrow ankles

Light, agile silhouette

No style adds bulk, weight, or heaviness.

Rule 3 — Expression Control

Facial emotion must match the style:

Sad only when required (Style 8)

Calm/confident elsewhere

No accidental grief in strong styles

Emotion is intentional, never default.

Rule 4 — Practical Realism

Every outfit must:

Make sense for the location

Make sense for the activity

Make sense for Australia’s climate

No fashion-for-fashion’s-sake.

3️⃣ STYLE TYPES
OUTFIT STYLES

These define clothing:

Styles 1–5

Styles 8–15

STATE STYLES

These define behaviour, not clothing:

6 (Protective)

7 (Action / Crisis)

State styles override posture, movement, and magic — not outfits.

4️⃣ COMPLETE STYLE DEFINITIONS (1–15)
1️⃣ Academy Uniform

Use:
Classes, assemblies, exams

Look:
Standard regulated academy uniform

Meaning:
Blending in. Obedience. Structure.

Rule:
Never expressive or personalised.

2️⃣ Modified Uniform

Use:
After hours, restricted areas

Look:
Subtle alterations (open jacket, swapped layers)

Meaning:
Quiet rebellion. Personal agency.

Rule:
Still recognisably a uniform.

3️⃣ Sneaking Out

Use:
Night exploration, secrecy

Look:
PJs + jacket, thrown together

Meaning:
Risk over comfort.

Rule:
Never looks planned.

4️⃣ Dorm / Private Space

Use:
Dorm room, late-night talks

Look:
Sleepwear, cropped allowed, barefoot

Meaning:
Safety, vulnerability.

Rule:
Only place emotional softness is fully visible.

5️⃣ Casual Day

Use:
Free time, off-campus

Look:
Simple, everyday clothes

Meaning:
Normal life.

Rule:
Should feel boring — intentionally.

6️⃣ Protective (STATE)

Use:
Anticipating danger

Look:
Whatever she’s wearing already

Meaning:
Instinct awakens early.

Rule:
Posture and positioning change — not clothes.

7️⃣ Action / Crisis (STATE)

Use:
Danger happening now

Look:
Current outfit, possibly damaged

Meaning:
Pure survival.

Rule:
Messiness is allowed. Speed over control.

8️⃣ Mourning / After Loss

Use:
After death or disappearance

Look:
Dark colours, minimal effort

Meaning:
Grief without words.

Rule:
Emotion overrides appearance.

9️⃣ Structured / Composed

Use:
Public life after loss

Look:
Clean lines, controlled silhouette

Meaning:
Holding it together.

Rule:
Never reads as mourning.

🔟 Refined / Transitional

Use:
Growth phase

Look:
Layered, intentional, slightly unsettled

Meaning:
Identity shifting.

Rule:
Confidence is forming, not finished.

1️⃣1️⃣ Quiet Magic / Memory

Use:
Reflection, remembrance

Look:
Simple clothes, subtle magic

Meaning:
Memory becomes power.

Rule:
Magic enhances emotion, never spectacle.

1️⃣2️⃣ Confident / Self-Owned

Use:
Self-assured moments

Look:
Feminine, clean, sharp

Meaning:
“I know who I am.”

Rule:
No sadness. No defensiveness.

1️⃣3️⃣ Formal / Ceremony

Use:
Ball, ceremony, public recognition

Look:
Formal gown or dress

Meaning:
Strength without armour.

Rule:
Rare and meaningful.

1️⃣4️⃣ Beach / Coastal Casual

Use:
Beach days, coastal towns

Look:
Sporty, cropped, thongs, barefoot

Meaning:
Freedom. Australia.

Rule:
Relaxed, never styled.

1️⃣5️⃣ Beach / In Water

Use:
Swimming, ocean scenes

Look:
Functional swimwear

Meaning:
Normal life still exists.

Rule:
Realism over glamour.

5️⃣ STYLE → STORY ARC LOGIC

Act I: 1, 2, 5, 14

Act II: 2, 3, 6

Act III: 8, 7

Act IV: 9, 10, 12

Act V: 12, 13, 15

Styles disappear and return to show growth.

6️⃣ CONTRAST RULE (MOST IMPORTANT)

Powerful scenes happen when:

The style does not match the danger

Examples:

Formal → Action

Dorm → Confrontation

Beach → Bad news

This creates emotional shock.

7️⃣ TRIO INTEGRATION RULE

Protector’s styles change least

Main character’s styles change most

Strategist’s styles become more practical

Visual growth mirrors emotional growth.

8️⃣ AUSTRALIAN IDENTITY RULE

Climate matters

Beach matters

Casual wear is normal

Over-formality feels rare

Setting must always be felt.

9️⃣ FUTURE EXPANSION RULE

New styles may be added only if they:

Serve story purpose

Fill a missing emotional or situational role

Do not overlap existing styles

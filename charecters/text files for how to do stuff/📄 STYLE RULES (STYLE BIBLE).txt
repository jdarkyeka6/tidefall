📄 STYLE RULES
Purpose

These rules exist to:

keep characters visually consistent

prevent tone drift

make style choices meaningful, not random

guide future art, writing, and game scenes

If something breaks a rule, it should feel intentional, not accidental.

1️⃣ CORE CONSISTENCY RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
1. Same character, always

Across all styles:

Same face

Same body type

Same height

Same general age appearance

Styles change context, not identity.

2. Body type lock

Slim athletic build

Lean legs

Narrow ankles

Light, agile silhouette

No style makes her visibly heavier or bulkier.

3. Expression follows style

Sad only when the style calls for it (e.g. Style 8)

Neutral or confident otherwise

No accidental sadness in confident styles

Face emotion must match style meaning.

4. No “fashion drift”

Outfits must feel:

practical for the moment

emotionally appropriate

consistent with personality

No random glamour or trend chasing.

2️⃣ STYLE CATEGORIES (IMPORTANT)
A. OUTFIT STYLES

Styles that define what she wears:

1–5

8–15

These are selectable looks.

B. STATE STYLES

Styles that define how she acts, not clothes:

6 (Protective)

7 (Action / Crisis)

These override posture, movement, and magic — not outfits.

3️⃣ STYLE-BY-STYLE RULES (SUMMARY FORM)
1 — Academy Uniform

Neat

Regulated

Suppressed individuality

Rule: never overly expressive.

2 — Modified Uniform

Small personal changes

Still recognisably uniform

Rule: rebellion is subtle, not loud.

3 — Sneaking Out

Comfort + urgency

Not styled

Rule: clothes chosen for speed, not looks.

4 — Dorm / Private Space

Safe

Soft

Cropped allowed

Sleepwear logic

Rule: vulnerability allowed here only.

5 — Casual Day

Normal life

Simple

Australian-appropriate

Rule: should feel “boringly normal”.

6 — Protective (STATE)

Anticipation

Guarding behaviour

Rule: outfit unchanged, posture changes.

7 — Action / Crisis (STATE)

Instinct

Speed

No hesitation

Rule: style chaos is acceptable here.

8 — Mourning / After Loss

Darker tones

Minimal effort

Quiet silhouette

Rule: emotion > appearance.

9 — Structured / Composed

Public facing

Controlled

Holding it together

Rule: never reads as mourning.

10 — Refined / Transitional

Growth phase

Between identities

Rule: looks intentional but unsettled.

11 — Quiet Magic / Memory

Subtle magic only

Reflection

Rule: magic enhances emotion, not spectacle.

12 — Confident / Self-Owned

Feminine, clean, sharp

Calm confidence

Rule: never sad, never defensive.

13 — Formal / Ceremony

Elegant

Composed

Rare usage

Rule: armour is removed here.

14 — Beach / Coastal Casual

Australian identity

Sporty, relaxed

Cropped allowed

Rule: freedom, not fashion.

15 — Beach / In Water

Functional swimwear

Natural movement

Rule: realism over stylisation.

4️⃣ STYLE TRANSITION RULES

Styles change because of:

location

time of day

emotional shift

story event

They do not change randomly.

5️⃣ STYLE CONTRAST RULE

High-impact scenes happen when:

the style does not match the danger

Example:

Style 13 + Style 7

Style 4 + Style 7

Style 15 + bad news

This is intentional tension.

6️⃣ AUSTRALIAN WORLD RULES

Climate matters

Beach matters

Casual wear is culturally normal

Over-formal looks should feel rare

7️⃣ FUTURE MAIN CHARACTER RULE

The main character’s styles must:

contrast this character’s choices

NOT mirror her exactly

reflect their personality differences

(This is why we waited.)

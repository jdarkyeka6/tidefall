📘 CHARACTER USE BOOK — JASPER

(Main Character / Choice-Maker)

CORE ROLE

Narrative centre

Moral weight carrier

Decision focal point

Story momentum anchor

PERSONALITY CORE

Thoughtful

Cautious, not cowardly

Empathetic

Self-questioning

Values intent over outcome (early)

Learns to accept outcome over intent (late)

WHAT BROKE HIM (UNSEEN)

Made a careful, morally “right” choice

Outcome still caused harm

Proved that thinking ≠ safety

Internalised responsibility for consequences

PRIMARY FEAR

Choosing wrong again

Acting without certainty

Making a decision alone

GUILT RESPONSE

Replays decisions mentally

Seeks confirmation from others

Delays commitment

Questions his authority to choose

HOW HE MAKES DECISIONS

Weighs options internally

Hesitates at forks

Looks to Harper for action signals

Looks to Ava for logic

Commits later in the story, earlier under pressure

HOW HE ACTS UNDER PRESSURE

Freezes briefly

Then commits fully

Accepts consequences once chosen

Carries guilt quietly afterward

HOW HE SHOWS EMOTION

Silence

Eye contact avoidance

Short answers

Physical stillness

Rare emotional outbursts (high impact)

HOW TO USE HIM IN SCENES

Put decisions in his hands

Make him aware of consequences before choosing

Force him to choose with incomplete information

Show hesitation before commitment

Let silence build tension

WHEN HE SHOULD SPEAK

Moral debates

Choosing between two harms

After something irreversible

When authority figures fail to decide

WHEN HE SHOULD BE QUIET

During fast action (Harper leads)

During planning (Ava leads)

During emotional fallout (others act around him)

HOW HE CREATES CONFLICT

Slows Harper down

Questions Ava’s certainty

Refuses “easy” choices

Tries to take responsibility for outcomes

WHAT HE WOULD NEVER FORGIVE

Being forced to choose between harms by someone avoiding responsibility

Authority figures who shift consequences onto others

Moral cowardice disguised as neutrality

WHAT HE CAN FORGIVE

Mistakes made in fear

Bad outcomes from good intent

Emotional outbursts

Being wrong

HIS RELATIONSHIP WITH HARPER

Relies on her instinct

Resents being overprotected (later)

Trusts her physically

Learns to push back to gain agency

HIS RELATIONSHIP WITH AVA

Respects her intelligence

Sometimes ignores her logic

Feels guilty when acting against her advice

Learns that logic doesn’t remove risk

HOW HE GROWS

Stops waiting for perfect information

Accepts uncertainty as unavoidable

Commits even when afraid

Learns that leadership includes failure

STYLE USAGE NOTES

Early story: blends into uniforms

Middle story: transitional styles dominate

Late story: Style 12 appears naturally

Style mismatch moments hit hardest with him

NEVER WRITE HIM AS

Reckless

Action-first hero

Emotionally detached

Always confident

Instantly decisive

QUICK SCENE CHECK (JASPER)

Before finalising a scene, ask:

Did Jasper have to choose?

Did the choice cost something?

Did hesitation affect the outcome?

If no → the scene probably isn’t using him correctly.

KEY THEMATIC FUNCTION

Jasper represents the cost of choice.

He is not meant to be comfortable to write.
If he feels heavy, he’s working.

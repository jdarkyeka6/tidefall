1️⃣ Lachlan Vale

Role: Pressure Mirror
Vibe: Calm · Loyal · Dangerous when pushed

Applies Pressure To: Harper

Protects his own the way Harper does

Forces moral symmetry

Sometimes right, sometimes terrifying

Story Use:
Makes fights feel unavoidable, not evil.

2️⃣ Isla Monroe

Role: Social System Player
Vibe: Polite · Believable · Untouchable

Applies Pressure To: Ava

Weaponises reputation and rules

Hurts without being blamed

Always looks reasonable

Story Use:
Teaches the player that systems hurt quietly.

3️⃣ Theo Kim

Role: Chaos Variable
Vibe: Brilliant · Unstable · Fearless

Applies Pressure To: Everyone

Breaks planning

Forces snap decisions

Can become ally or disaster

Story Use:
Unpredictable turning points.

4️⃣ Mia Tran

Role: Innocent Stake
Vibe: Talented · Quiet · Invisible

Applies Pressure To: Jasper

5️⃣ Benji Rowan

Vibe: Funny · Nervous · Survival-first

🔑 Core Rule (FINAL VERSION)

The player never thinks as Ava —
they learn to respect her later.

Early on, Ava feels optional.
Mid-game, ignoring her hurts.
Late-game, ignoring her reshapes the story.

That’s the arc.

🔁 Final Choice Pipeline

Situation appears

Player (Jasper) reads the room

Harper pushes for action

Ava comments sometimes

Player chooses

Consequences land later

Ava speaks less than Harper, always.

🛡️ Harper’s Choice Function (PRIMARY)

Harper:

Triggers timers

Forces urgency

Makes waiting dangerous

Mechanically:

Short decision windows

Escalation on hesitation

Clear immediate risk

Harper is what the player feels.

🧠 Ava’s Choice Function (SECONDARY)

Ava:

Does not interrupt every choice

Only speaks when systems are affected

Focuses on future stability

Ava Line Examples:

“This creates a pattern.”

“We don’t get this choice twice.”

“They’ll remember this.”

No numbers. No predictions.

She is context, not guidance.

⚠️ Ava Warning Levels (FINALIZED)
🟢 Soft Comment (Early Game)

Optional

Ignoring is safe

Teaches tone

🟡 Firm Comment (Mid Game)

Signals lasting impact

Ignoring hurts later

🔴 Hard Line (Late Game)

Ava states permanence

Routes lock

Ava may disengage later

Still rare. Still powerful.

🤫 Ava Silence Rule (IMPORTANT)

Most choices have no Ava comment at all.

This keeps:

Her credibility intact

The player from gaming her warnings

Harper in the foreground

🧠 Example (Correct POV)
Situation:

Breaking school rules to protect someone.

Harper:

“We don’t have time to argue.”

Ava:

“Once we cross this line, it’s easier next time.”

Player (Jasper) chooses action.

Later:

If ignored → escalation arc

If obeyed → slower, safer progression

The player connects the dots themselves.

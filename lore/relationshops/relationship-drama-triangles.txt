🔺 JEALOUSY TRIANGLES (DESIGNED FOR DRAMA)

Each triangle includes:

Core tension

What triggers jealousy

How it escalates

How it resolves (or doesn’t)

🔥 TRIANGLE 1: Jasper 💙 – Harper 🩷 – Ava 🩷

Type: Control vs Trust vs Foresight
Intensity: 💥💥💥💥💥 (S-tier)

Core Tension

Harper believes action = safety

Ava believes planning = survival

Jasper is caught choosing between force and foresight

Jealousy Trigger

Ava starts advising Jasper privately.
Harper notices decisions changing — slower, calculated.

Escalation

Harper challenges Ava openly

Ava frames Harper as reckless

Jasper hesitates trying to keep the peace → makes it worse

Fallout

Harper steps in without approval

Ava says: “This is why planning matters.”

Jasper feels both protected and undermined

Resolution Paths

💔 Jasper sides with Harper → Ava withdraws trust

🧠 Jasper sides with Ava → Harper feels replaced

🔥 Jasper asserts leadership → both recalibrate (best ending)

🧠 TRIANGLE 2: Jasper 💙 – Ava 🩷 – Keira 🩷

Type: Logic vs Morality
Intensity: 💥💥💥💥

Core Tension

Ava accepts moral cost

Keira rejects compromise

Jasper lives between them

Jealousy Trigger

Jasper supports a necessary-but-unjust decision Ava predicted.

Escalation

Keira accuses Ava of being cold

Ava accuses Keira of being naïve

Jasper is forced to explain himself — repeatedly

Fallout

Keira feels emotionally sidelined.
Ava feels morally misrepresented.

Resolution Paths

⚡ Jasper defends the choice → Keira respects conviction

❌ Jasper apologizes too much → both lose respect

🧠 Ava admits uncertainty → humanizes her, defuses triangle

🛡️ TRIANGLE 3: Jasper 💙 – Harper 🩷 – Isla 🩷

Type: Private loyalty vs Public loyalty
Intensity: 💥💥💥

Core Tension

Harper stands beside Jasper in danger

Isla stands with him in public

Each believes they’re the one who truly supports him

Jealousy Trigger

Isla publicly distances herself during controversy.
Harper notices immediately.

Escalation

Harper calls Isla out for “cowardice”

Isla reframes it as strategy

Jasper is defended privately, abandoned publicly

Fallout

Jasper questions who actually has his back.

Resolution Paths

🎭 Isla makes a public stand → triangle collapses

🗡️ Harper escalates conflict → Isla withdraws

💔 Jasper tolerates it → long-term trust damage

❤️ TRIANGLE 4: Raf 💙 – Mia 🩷 – Jasper 💙

Type: Emotional safety vs Hero gravity
Intensity: 💥💥💥

Core Tension

Raf offers steady emotional presence

Jasper represents danger + intensity

Mia is pulled toward both

Jealousy Trigger

Mia seeks Jasper during a crisis instead of Raf.

Escalation

Raf feels replaced but doesn’t confront

Mia senses guilt

Jasper feels responsible and pulls back

Fallout

Everyone feels wrong for different reasons.

Resolution Paths

❤️ Mia chooses Raf → stability, slower pace

🌱 Mia chooses independence → healthiest growth

💥 Jasper intervenes emotionally → creates dependence (bad)

🌙 TRIANGLE 5: Lachlan 💙 – Isla 🩷 – Theo 💙

Type: Stability vs Chaos
Intensity: 💥💥💥

Core Tension

Lachlan offers safety and silence

Theo offers excitement and visibility

Isla is tempted by spectacle

Jealousy Trigger

Theo draws public attention Isla enjoys.
Lachlan notices — says nothing.

Escalation

Isla pushes for more visibility

Lachlan withdraws

Theo escalates behavior to keep attention

Fallout

Isla realizes excitement costs stability.

Resolution Paths

🌙 Isla chooses Lachlan → quiet but strong

🎢 Isla chooses Theo → short, loud collapse

❌ Isla tries both → loses both

♟️ TRIANGLE 6: Ava 🩷 – Noah 💙 – Isla 🩷

Type: Strategy vs Influence
Intensity: 💥💥

Core Tension

Ava wants truth

Isla wants perception

Noah benefits from both

Jealousy Trigger

Noah shares different “versions” of plans with each.

Escalation

Isla senses manipulation socially

Ava notices inconsistencies logically

Noah tries to balance instead of choosing

Fallout

Trust fractures asymmetrically.

Resolution Paths

♟️ Noah chooses Ava → loses social leverage

🎭 Noah chooses Isla → loses strategic depth

❌ Noah chooses neither → both leave

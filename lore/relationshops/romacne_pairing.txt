Jasper 💙 × Harper 🩷 — 9.5/10 🔥

Core Dynamic:
Instinct vs agency. Harper protects by acting; Jasper leads by choosing.

Why it works:
They trust each other under pressure more than anyone else. Harper believes Jasper will stand when it matters. Jasper believes Harper will never abandon him in danger.

Primary Conflict:
Harper starts overriding Jasper’s decisions when she thinks he’s hesitating. Jasper begins to feel controlled instead of supported.

Failure State:
Harper makes a “necessary” decision for Jasper in public. Jasper feels his agency erased.

Recovery Path:
Jasper makes a dangerous call Harper disagrees with — and she backs him anyway. That choice permanently rebalances them.

Jasper 💙 × Ava 🩷 — 9/10 🧠

Core Dynamic:
Heart vs foresight. Jasper chooses in the moment; Ava sees the long arc.

Why it works:
Deep intellectual respect turns into intimacy. Ava trusts Jasper’s instincts; Jasper trusts Ava’s planning.

Primary Conflict:
Ava optimizes outcomes that emotionally wound Jasper. Jasper feels “managed” instead of chosen.

Failure State:
Ava withholds the full truth to ensure the “correct” outcome.

Recovery Path:
Ava gives Jasper full information knowing he might choose differently — and accepts the result.

📘 RELATIONSHIP RULES (GLOBAL CANON)
CORE PHILOSOPHY

Relationships are systems, not rewards.

No relationship exists just to please the player

No relationship is guaranteed to succeed

All relationships remember past behaviour

Every relationship can strain, fail, and recover

Nothing resets to default.

🔗 TYPES OF RELATIONSHIPS

Every relationship fits one primary category:

Loyalty-based (Raf, Harper)

Trust-based (Ava, Isla, Lachlan)

Emotion-based (Mia, Benji)

Morality-based (Keira, Talia)

Transactional (Noah)

Observational (Soren)

A relationship can shift category, but only after a major event.

📈 RELATIONSHIP STATES (NOT LINEAR)

Relationships do not move cleanly upward.

Possible states:

Neutral

Growing

Stable

Strained

Toxic

Broken

Recovering

Broken ≠ ended
Toxic ≠ evil
Recovery is always possible, but never free

⚠️ FAILURE RULES (VERY IMPORTANT)

A relationship can fail if:

The player repeats the same mistake

Apologies are not backed by action

The character is ignored during a crisis

The player chooses outcomes over people (for the wrong character)

Failure does not mean:

The character disappears

The character becomes hostile by default

Content is permanently locked

Failure means:

The relationship changes shape

🔄 RECOVERY RULES

Recovery requires three things (all mandatory):

Acknowledgment
The player must admit what they did, not just that it hurt.

Cost
Recovery must require sacrifice:

time

opportunity

power

reputation

Changed behaviour
The same mistake cannot be made again during recovery.

Skipping any step = fake recovery (relationship remains unstable).

❤️ ROMANCE-SPECIFIC RULES

Romance is a major emotional driver, not side content.

Romance can:

Fail

Become toxic

Turn cold

Recover later

Permanently redefine characters

Romance CANNOT:

Be consequence-free

Override personality rules

Ignore power imbalance

Progress during emotional avoidance

💔 TOXICITY RULES

A relationship becomes toxic when:

Protection becomes control (Harper)

Logic replaces empathy (Ava)

Loyalty is taken for granted (Raf)

Fear governs decisions (Benji)

Morality becomes self-righteousness (Keira)

Toxic phases are:

Uncomfortable

Tense

Playable

Intentional

They are not jokes. They must be addressed or they worsen.

🧠 MEMORY & DELAYED CONSEQUENCES

Some characters react immediately.
Some react later.

Examples:

Soren reacts late but decisively

Isla reacts socially, not directly

Ava logs inconsistencies silently

Harper reacts instantly, emotionally

Just because nothing happened yet
does not mean nothing will happen.

🎭 PUBLIC VS PRIVATE BEHAVIOUR

Relationships behave differently based on context:

Public betrayal ≠ private betrayal

Public defense matters

Silence in public can damage trust more than private failure

NPCs remember who saw what.

🧑‍🏫 TEACHER RELATIONSHIP RULES

Teachers:

Do not engage in romance

Can mentor, protect, oppose, or restrict

Change slowly

React strongly to repeated patterns

Trust with teachers affects:

information access

rule enforcement

protection during crises

🔒 HARD LIMITS (DO NOT BREAK)
NEVER:
- Auto-forgive the player
- Use romance as a reward
- Erase past conflict
- Make everyone like Jasper by default
- Let relationships ignore character rules

ALLOWED:
- Forgiveness after cost
- Distance without hostility
- Bittersweet endings
- Relationships that end respectfully

🎯 DESIGN CHECK (USE THIS CONSTANTLY)

Before writing a relationship scene, ask:

What does this cost?

Who remembers this later?

Does this reinforce who the character is?

Would this feel different with a different character?

If the answer is “no difference” → rewrite.

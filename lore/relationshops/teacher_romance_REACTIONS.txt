🧑‍🏫 TEACHER REACTIONS TO STUDENT ROMANCES (CANON)
HOW TO USE THIS

For any romance scene, check:

Visibility (public / private)

Performance impact (grades, combat, safety)

Behaviour shift (control, recklessness, withdrawal)

Teachers react to effects, not feelings.

🎓 ALARIC THORNE — Strategic Tolerance

Default Stance: Observational, non-intervention
Threshold to Act: When romance destabilizes leadership or timing

Reacts Positively When:

Romance improves judgment under pressure

Partners challenge each other productively

Conflict stays contained

Reacts Negatively When:

Romance drives impulsive public action

Secrets compromise institutional timing

Interventions:

Withholds information

Reassigns responsibilities quietly

One-on-one warnings framed as mentorship

Notes:
Thorne allows romance to exist until it threatens outcomes. He never forbids — he delays.

🔒 HARD RULES (GLOBAL)
NEVER:
- Teachers gossip
- Teachers shame affection itself
- Teachers act instantly without pattern

ALWAYS:
- Teachers react to outcomes
- Consequences scale with visibility
- Private behaviour matters less than public fallout

🎯 DESIGN CHECK

If a teacher reacts:

ask why now?

identify the pattern

apply the lightest effective pressure

Overreaction breaks realism.

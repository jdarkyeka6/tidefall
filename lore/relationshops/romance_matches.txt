❤️ ROMANCE MATRICES (WHO CLASHES WITH WHO)
HOW TO READ THIS

Each pairing includes:

Attraction Type – why they’re drawn together

Core Clash – what always causes friction

Toxic Risk – how it can go wrong

Recovery Path – how it can be saved

Not every pairing must be playable — but all are narratively valid.

🧍 JASPER VALE (Player)
❤️ Jasper × Harper

Attraction:
Protection, shared history, trust under fire

Core Clash:
Control vs autonomy

Toxic Risk:
Harper overrides Jasper’s choices “for his own good”

Recovery Path:
Jasper asserts agency in a dangerous moment and Harper lets him

❤️ Jasper × Ava

Attraction:
Mind-to-mind respect, quiet intimacy

Core Clash:
Logic vs emotional risk

Toxic Risk:
Ava calculates outcomes that hurt Jasper personally

Recovery Path:
Ava chooses uncertainty and lets Jasper decide

❤️ Jasper × Keira

Attraction:
Moral intensity, honesty

Core Clash:
Idealism vs pragmatism

Toxic Risk:
Keira judges Jasper’s compromises harshly

Recovery Path:
Jasper openly defends a morally costly choice without hiding

❤️ Jasper × Mia

Strength imbalance

Mia becomes emotionally dependent

Jasper steps back and lets Mia fail safely

Reputation vs truth

Isla distances herself to protect image

🧠 OBSERVER CASE
⚠️ Soren × Anyone

Rule:
Soren does not engage until late story.

If romance occurs:

It is quiet

It references past actions accurately

It ends truthfully, not dramatically

🔒 HARD GLOBAL RULES
NEVER:
- Force symmetry (not all pairings equal)
- Let romance bypass character rules
- Let toxic phases resolve without cost

ALLOWED:
- Bittersweet endings
- Failed romances that still matter
- Growth through loss

🎯 DESIGN INTENT

This matrix ensures:

Conflict stays character-driven

Romance adds pressure, not comfort

The world reacts authentically

If a pairing feels “easy” — it’s wrong.

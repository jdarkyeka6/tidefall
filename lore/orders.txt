📜 The Orders of Tidefall

(Core Structure of Student Life at the Academy)

Tidefall does not divide its students by bloodline, gender, talent, or background.

Instead, every student is inducted into an Order — a centuries-old academic and philosophical grouping that governs how students live, are mentored, and are challenged.

An Order is not a measure of worth.
It is a framework.

Students of every Order study together in classes, train together, and compete together — but they live, sleep, and are mentored within their Order.

🏰 What an Order Defines

Each Order determines:

🛏️ Dormitory wing (mixed-gender, grouped by year)

🧑‍🏫 Primary mentors & tutors

🧠 Philosophical approach to magic

⚖️ Internal expectations and discipline style

🔒 Restricted spaces & Order-only routes

An Order does not lock a student’s future — many of Tidefall’s greatest figures broke from their Order’s norms.

🔹 Order of Astrae

Motto: “To see clearly is to shape what comes next.”

Core Belief

Knowledge is power only when it is understood, contextualised, and anticipated.

Focus

Strategy

Research

Pattern recognition

Long-term planning

Reputation

Astrae students are seen as:

Quietly influential

Rarely surprised

Often several steps ahead

They are trusted with maps, records, and future-facing decisions.

Living Spaces

Upper halls near archives and observatories

Layered floors with private study nooks

Restricted map rooms and sealed libraries

Strengths

Strategic thinking

Measured responses

High academic success

Weaknesses

Can overthink

May hesitate to act

Risk becoming detached

🔹 Order of Brannor

Motto: “Strength exists to protect.”

Core Belief

Power is meaningless unless it is used to defend others.

Focus

Endurance

Courage

Defensive magic

Leadership under pressure

Reputation

Brannor students are:

Trusted in crisis

Physically disciplined

Often placed at the front when things go wrong

They are respected even by those who disagree with them.

Living Spaces

Lower towers and reinforced wings

Training halls and sparring courts

Banner-lined common rooms

Strengths

Loyalty

Physical and magical resilience

Decisiveness

Weaknesses

Can be stubborn

May carry too much responsibility

Struggle with ambiguity

🔹 Order of Calyx

Motto: “Change is not chaos — it is opportunity.”

Core Belief

Adaptability is the most powerful form of intelligence.

Focus

Creative magic

Social intelligence

Improvisation

Reading people and situations

Reputation

Calyx students are known as:

Clever negotiators

Rule-benders rather than rule-breakers

Unpredictable, but effective

They often succeed in situations where rigid plans fail.

Living Spaces

Open-plan dorm floors

Large shared common halls

Informal meeting areas

Strengths

Flexibility

Persuasion

Innovation

Weaknesses

Inconsistency

Impulsiveness

Can underestimate consequences

🔹 Order of Nocten

Motto: “Time reveals all truths.”

Core Belief

Lasting power comes from patience, discipline, and memory.

Focus

Long-term strategy

Precision magic

Control and restraint

Consequence-based decision making

Reputation

Nocten students are:

Quietly intimidating

Respected and watched carefully

Known for thinking in years, not days

They are often assigned to sensitive or morally complex tasks.

Living Spaces

Uppermost towers and silent corridors

Restricted access floors

Minimalist, controlled environments

Strengths

Focus

Discipline

Strategic endurance

Weaknesses

Emotional distance

Rigidity

Difficulty adapting quickly

🧍 The Trio’s Place in the System

Jasper, Ava, and Harper are inducted into the same Order.

This is intentional.

Tidefall’s philosophy holds that:

“True capability is revealed when different strengths are tested under the same expectations.”

Their shared Order becomes:

A pressure cooker

A proving ground

A source of loyalty and conflict

They are not divided by system — they are tested within it.
